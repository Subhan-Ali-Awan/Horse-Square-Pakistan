# Workspace Rules for Horse-Square-Pakistan

## Persistent Horse Listings Policy
- When a user logs in, fills out the form to sell a horse, and submits the listing: once approved and displayed in the browse marketplace section, the listing MUST remain persistently stored in the database.
- Logging out of the website MUST NOT delete, hide, or alter any published horse listings.
- Horse listings MUST remain published and visible in the marketplace listing section permanently until explicitly deleted by an Admin (or by the listing owner via their dashboard).
